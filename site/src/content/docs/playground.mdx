Try a PPTX, XLSX or DOCX from the examples, or select your own local file.
Your choice follows you between tabs; local files stay in your browser and
are not uploaded. All four integrations use the same renderers.

New here? Start with **HTML element** for built-in controls. The other tabs
show a minimal mount example beside a live viewer. Their paging and zoom bars
are app-owned demo UI, not controls automatically created by those snippets;
[add your own controls](../start/#build-your-own-toolbar) when you need them.

This is a view-only preview: no editing, conversion or formula recalculation.
[Getting started](../start/) covers installation and host sizing;
[fidelity and limits](../api/#fidelity-is-part-of-the-contract) describes what to expect.
